Frequently asked questions

What image formats can Squish compress?
HEIC, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, and SVG. It can also convert between most of these formats in a single step.
How much can Squish reduce image file size?
Typically 40–80%. A 5 MB HEIC photo from an iPhone can often be reduced to under 500 KB as a JPEG without visible quality loss.
What's the difference between HEIC, JPEG, and WebP?
HEIC is Apple's format for iPhone photos — excellent quality but not universally supported. JPEG is the universal standard for photos. WebP is a modern format with better compression than JPEG and is supported by all major browsers.
Is there a file size limit?
No server-side limits — all processing runs in your browser. Very large images may take longer on low-powered devices, but there's no cap.
Can I compress multiple images at once?
Yes — drop multiple files at once and Squish processes them all in parallel, then lets you download individually or as a ZIP.
Are my images safe?
Completely. Squish uses WebAssembly to process images entirely in your browser. No image is ever sent to a server.
